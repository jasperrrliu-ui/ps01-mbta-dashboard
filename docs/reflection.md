# Reflection

The data supports a descriptive comparison of recorded service-impact alerts and comparable observed schedule differences over a clearly defined 90-day period. It supports the finding that Red and Blue had more service-impact alerts than the other subway lines in this snapshot, and that Red and Blue had a larger share of comparable observed stops more than five minutes late.

The data cannot prove that a particular line is inherently unreliable, that every rider experienced a delay, or why the patterns occurred. Alerts are published administrative records, and performance records do not measure passengers, crowding, weather, construction, or every missed connection. The dashboard therefore separates the observed measures from any claim of cause and presents them as evidence for planning and further investigation.
