The map is deliberately muted so your route is the reading layer. Red station rings flag historical vehicle schedule deviation; amber marks a transfer with its measured scheduled-connection result.

What the time estimate means: observed excess boarding headway, excess in-vehicle travel time, and transfer wait after a three-minute walk. A missed connection means the scheduled outgoing train had already left after the incoming train arrived.

Study window: June 22-September 19, 2026. This 90-day summer snapshot is descriptive, not a year-round calibrated prediction.

Sources: MBTA LAMP public data and the official MBTA subway map. Data limitations and methods are documented in the project repository.
