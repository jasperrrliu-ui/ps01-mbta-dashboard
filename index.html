About the evidence

A planning tool for Boston subway riders

This Site helps riders decide when a trip needs extra time and where a transfer may fail. It combines official MBTA stop-performance records with scheduled service; it describes historical conditions rather than predicting a particular train.

How to use it

  1. Compare the same trip across available travel hours.
  2. Use the added-time estimate as planning evidence, not a guarantee.
  3. Check current MBTA notices before a time-critical departure.

Important limitations

  • The 90-day window is a summer snapshot, not a year-round calibration.
  • Transfer estimates assume a three-minute walk and random rider arrival.
  • The data does not measure crowding, weather, rider volume, or causation.
